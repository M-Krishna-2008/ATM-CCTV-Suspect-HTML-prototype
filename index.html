How It Works: 8-Stage Offline Forensic Video Pipeline

The pipeline operates completely on an air-gapped, local workstation. High-resolution ATM video is ingested, synchronized with bank transaction logs, and filtered down to the highest-clarity faces using biometric quality metrics.

Underlying Production Stack

• Video Ingestion: FFmpeg libavformat / libavcodec with direct hardware NVDEC decoding.
• Tracking: YOLO11m + BoT-SORT running in FP16 TensorRT for camera jitter resilience.
• Face Localization: SCRFD-10G (wide-angle banking lens distortion calibrated).
• Embeddings & Search: AdaFace + ArcFace (512-d normalized vectors) indexed in local FAISS HNSW flat indices.
• Audit Ledger: Embedded SQLite with SHA-256 chain of custody cryptographic sealing.
